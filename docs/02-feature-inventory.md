## Problems and recommendations

The status is intentionally not collapsed into “frontend exists = working.” Before demonstration, prioritize photo completion, role/ownership/state correctness, truthful AI failure states and data labels. For remaining untested branches, use [the verification queue](10-functional-audit.md) rather than assuming success.
